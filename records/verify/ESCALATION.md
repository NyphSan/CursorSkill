Equally, call out when NO pattern is warranted and a plain implementation is better. Not for line-level code style or formatting (that is the conventions layer), general non-Unreal pattern theory with no UE context, or Blueprint-only visual scripting. Complements the unreal-solid skill: patterns are how you realize the SOLID principles.
